Innovation

The Story Behind the Laally Bridge

During her clinical work, Kate identified a critical gap in care for NICU babies: the transition from tube feeding to breastfeeding. Many premature or medically fragile infants struggled to make this leap — and families were left without a reliable bridge between the two.

Kate invented the Laally Bridge to fill that gap — a device designed specifically to support the oral feeding progression of NICU infants, helping them build the suckling patterns needed for successful breastfeeding.

The Laally Bridge is now commercially available as the Neotech Bridge, manufactured and distributed by Neotech Products. It is used in NICUs across the country to support some of the most vulnerable newborns in their earliest days of life.

The Neotech Bridge

Originally developed as the Laally Bridge by Kate Spivak, the Neotech Bridge is now a commercially available medical device used in neonatal intensive care units to support the feeding development of premature and medically fragile infants.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an IBCLC?

An International Board Certified Lactation Consultant (IBCLC) is a healthcare professional who has met rigorous clinical and educational requirements and passed a comprehensive exam to specialize in breastfeeding and human lactation. IBCLCs are recognized as the highest credential in lactation care.

How is Kate different from a lactation counselor or peer support?

Kate holds both an IBCLC credential and a Physician Assistant license — a rare combination. This means she can identify and address clinical factors affecting breastfeeding (such as tongue tie, infant oral anatomy, or maternal conditions) in addition to providing feeding guidance and emotional support.

When should I reach out for help?

As early as possible — ideally before or right at birth. Common reasons to call include: painful latch, concerns about milk supply, engorgement, nipple pain, a baby who won't latch, or just wanting to get off to the best start. It's never too early or too late to ask for help.

Do you take insurance?

Many insurance plans cover lactation services. Contact your insurance provider to understand your benefits. Kate can provide documentation to support insurance reimbursement claims.

What areas do you serve?

In-home visits are available throughout Montgomery County and Bucks County, PA. Virtual consults are available to families anywhere in the country.
