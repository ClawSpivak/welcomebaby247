About Kate

A Clinician Who's Been in Your Corner

Kate Spivak is a Physician Assistant and International Board Certified Lactation Consultant (IBCLC) with deep clinical expertise and a genuine passion for supporting new families.

  • Physician Assistant (PA)
  • International Board Certified Lactation Consultant (IBCLC)
  • Inventor of the Laally Bridge, now sold commercially as the Neotech Bridge
  • Serving Montgomery & Bucks County families for years

The Bridge That Changed NICU Care

Kate invented the Laally Bridge — a device designed to help NICU babies transition from tube feeding to breastfeeding. It is now commercially available as the Neotech Bridge, used in NICUs across the country.
