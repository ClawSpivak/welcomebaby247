Services

In-home and virtual lactation support tailored to you and your baby.

In-Home Visits

Expert Support in the Comfort of Your Home

The most effective lactation support happens in your real environment — in the chair where you actually nurse, with the pillow you actually use. I come to you in Montgomery County and Bucks County, PA, so you never have to bundle up your newborn for an office visit.

A typical in-home visit includes a full feeding assessment, hands-on guidance with positioning and latch, evaluation of milk transfer, and a personalized care plan you can follow between appointments.

  • Full feeding assessment
  • Hands-on positioning & latch support
  • Infant weight check (with portable scale)
  • Milk transfer evaluation
  • Personalized written care plan
  • Follow-up support by email

Virtual Consults

Expert Guidance, Wherever You Are

Can't do an in-home visit? Virtual consults offer the same expert guidance through a secure video call. Ideal for families outside my in-home service area, follow-up appointments, or when leaving home just isn't feasible.

I can see your latch, assess your baby's positioning, walk you through pumping setup, answer your questions, and build a plan — all without you leaving your couch.

  • Secure video consult

  • Available to families anywhere
  • Flexible scheduling

Common Concerns

What I Can Help With

No issue is too small or too complicated. Here are some of the most common reasons families reach out.

😣

Pain & Discomfort

Nipple pain, cracking, bleeding, or general discomfort are not normal parts of breastfeeding. There is almost always a solvable cause.

📉

Low Milk Supply

Concerns about whether your baby is getting enough are extremely common. I can assess true supply, identify contributing factors, and build a plan.

🔗

Latch Difficulties

A poor latch is the root cause of many breastfeeding problems. I'll observe a full feeding and help you find what works for your baby.

👅

Tongue & Lip Tie

Tongue tie and lip tie can significantly impact feeding. I can assess for ties and coordinate with your pediatric team if a revision is needed.

🍼

Bottle & Combination Feeding

Introducing a bottle, supplementing with formula, or pumping — I'll help you combine feeding methods in a way that works for your family.

🏥

NICU & Special Circumstances

Premature birth, medical complications, or NICU stays add complexity to feeding. Kate's NICU expertise — including her work developing the Neotech Bridge — informs her approach to these cases.

What to Expect

Your First Appointment

Before the Visit

We'll talk briefly before your appointment to understand your situation and goals. Try not to feed your baby immediately before I arrive — ideally I'll be able to observe a full feeding from hunger cues through to the end.

During the Visit

I'll take a history, observe and assist with a feeding, assess your baby's weight and transfer if indicated, and address your specific concerns. In-home visits typically last 60–90 minutes.

After the Visit

You'll receive a written care plan summarizing our findings and recommendations. I'm available for follow-up questions by email, and we can schedule additional appointments as needed.

Insurance & Payment

Many insurance plans now cover lactation services under the ACA. I can provide documentation to support your reimbursement claim. Contact your insurance provider to verify your specific benefits.
